About me

Human Nutrition graduate with hands-on clinical and food-industry experience.

I'm Aleeza Naeem — a BS (Hons) Human Nutrition graduate from the University of Agriculture Faisalabad (CGPA 3.63). I've planned therapeutic diets in ICU wards and pediatric clinics, run quality audits at a Pepsi Cola plant, and taken my own food brand to the Annual Food and Nutrition Mela three times. I believe that good nutrition is built in honest conversation, not handed down as a list of rules.
